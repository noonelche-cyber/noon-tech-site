SPANISH WITH CONTEXT

Understand it before you say it.

Insults, slang and everyday expressions: discover what they mean, how they sound and when they can land badly. ¡JODER! explores Spanish from Spain and Latin America, with context instead of word-for-word guesswork.

01

More than a translation

Explore the real meaning, literal translation, examples and regional context. Search in Spanish or in your own language.

02

Know the intensity

Five levels help you distinguish a light jab from a serious insult. Tone, relationships and region can change how an expression is received.

03

Listen to the words

Hear expressions aloud and explore pronunciation. Adjust the voice and playback speed in the settings.

04

Learn through games

Translation quizzes, intensity challenges, missing words and the perfect reply: practise understanding the expression, not just memorising it.

05

Keep your favourites

Save expressions to find them again. Hide the ones you do not want to see in your feed.

06

A little Spanish, every day

The Android widget brings expressions to your home screen. Filters let you explore by level, category and region.

Curious ears. Better judgement.

For learners who want to understand films, conversations and the Spanish that textbooks leave out. Knowing an expression does not mean using it everywhere: context comes first.
